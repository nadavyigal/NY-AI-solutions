# How to start an engagement

Everything you need, in order. No theory. Each step says what you send or say,
and what has to be true before you move on.

Full detail lives in the files linked at each step. This page is the checklist.

---

## Step 0. Get the call

You need someone to talk to. That is the whole bottleneck right now.

Pick ten people you already know: insurance, law, accounting, real estate,
consulting. Ask for a 30-minute call, not for business.

What to send them:

> יש לי שירות חדש שאני בונה, ואני רוצה לבדוק אותו על עסקים אמיתיים.
> 30 דקות, בלי מצגת ובלי מכירה. אני רוצה להבין איך אתם עובדים היום ואיפה
> הזמן הולך. אם אני רואה משהו ששווה לשפר, אגיד. אם לא, אגיד גם את זה.

**Log every call** in [gtm/fit-call-log.md](gtm/fit-call-log.md), the moment it
happens. A message sent is not a call.

---

## Step 1. Send the intake form

Send it **before** the call. Ten minutes of their time.

The ten questions are in [toolkit/01-intake.md](toolkit/01-intake.md), under
"Client-facing form". Copy them into WhatsApp or email as they are.

Its only job: make the call about **one workflow** instead of about AI in general.

Also send the short Hebrew deck: `sales/NY-AI-Solutions-Service-Presentation-Hebrew-RTL-v5.pptx`.

## Step 2. Run the fit call, 30 to 45 minutes

Open with this, from [sales/NY-AI-Solutions-Service-Pitch-Hebrew-v5.md](sales/NY-AI-Solutions-Service-Pitch-Hebrew-v5.md):

> לפני שנדבר על כלים, אני רוצה להבין מה בעסק צריך לעבוד טוב יותר.
> אם השיחה הזו תצליח, איזו בעיה תרצה שנדע להגדיר בצורה ברורה?

Questions to work through: [sales/prospect-call-cheat-sheet-he.md](sales/prospect-call-cheat-sheet-he.md).

Close with this:

> אני שומע שהבעיה המרכזית היא ____. ההזדמנות הראשונה שנראית הגיונית היא ____.
> לפני שנבנה, צריך לבדוק את ____. הצעד הבא שאני מציע הוא ספרינט קצר שבו נמפה
> את התהליך, נבחן חלופות וסיכונים, ונבנה פתרון ראשון לבדיקה.

**You only continue if you can name one real workflow.** If you cannot, say so
and end it. An honest no is a valid outcome of the call and it protects the
offer.

---

## Step 3. Send the proposal

Only if step 2 named a workflow. Send:

1. The problem in one sentence, in their words.
2. The five things the sprint delivers ([service/offer.md](service/offer.md)).
3. Timeline: 7 to 10 business days.
4. Price. Decided per client, never published.

---

first engagement, and it still sat there, because standing it up needed the
client's own account and a scheduled hour nobody booked.

---

## The three things that must exist or the sprint is not done

1. **A measured baseline** (step 3). How long the workflow takes today, in
   minutes and handoffs. Without it you cannot claim any result later.
2. **A privacy path** for every workflow you propose (step 4). Where identifying
   data goes. Aliases in, the model never sees a name, the client re-attaches
   identities offline at the send step.
3. **A "leave this alone" finding** (step 4). Name one process that works and
   should not be touched. An audit that finds only opportunities reads as a
   sales document.

---

## Rules that stop you embarrassing yourself

- **Never claim a number you did not measure on both sides.** Baseline before,
  same measure after.
- **Check what tier they actually have, inside the account.** Do not trust the
  plan name. What the tier allows decides what you can design.
- **Inventory what they already pay for before proposing anything new.** New
  tools are allowed. They are not the opening move.
- **One workflow at a time.** Sprint, implementation and retainer are separate
  scopes with separate agreements.
