# NY AI Solutions — Value Proposition

Last updated: 2026-08-30. Supersedes nothing; sharpens EXD-026 (2026-08-11).

## The sentence

> **Your team already pays for AI. I make it actually do the work, inside the
> tools you already have, without your client data leaving your control.**

Hebrew:

> **אתם כבר משלמים על AI. אני גורם לו לעשות את העבודה בפועל, בתוך הכלים שכבר יש
> לכם, בלי שהמידע של הלקוחות שלכם יוצא מהשליטה שלכם.**

## Who this is for

Israeli professional-services businesses, 10 to 100 employees: insurance
agencies, law firms, finance and accounting practices, real estate, consulting.

The qualifying signal is not size. It is this shape:

- The business already pays for at least one AI subscription that nobody uses
  beyond drafting an email.
- The real work runs through a repeated workflow a human does by hand every week.
- The business holds client data it is not allowed to be careless with.

A business missing the third item does not need me specifically. That is the
segment where a generic automation vendor is a fine answer.

## The three claims, and what backs each

| Claim | Evidence | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| I work inside what you already pay for | Michal's operating system runs entirely on the Google AI Plus tier she already had (~₪19/mo). No new platform, no migration, no procurement cycle. | Not a refusal to recommend new tools. It is an ordering rule: existing subscriptions first, because that is where value is already paid for and unclaimed. |
| I build, I do not only advise | RunSmart and Resumely are both live on the App Store, built and shipped solo. The consulting is done by someone who ships production software. | Not a promise that every engagement produces custom software. Most produce configured workflows. |
| Client data stays under your control | The Michal engagement's privacy architecture: aliases go in, the AI never sees a client's name or identifying detail, real identities are re-attached by the client offline at the moment of sending. Designed before any automation was built. | Not a compliance certification, and not a legal opinion. It is an architecture practice. |

## Why the privacy claim is the wedge, not a footnote

For an insurance agency or a law firm, the reason AI adoption has stalled is
rarely curiosity. It is that nobody in the business can answer "where does the
client's data go" with confidence, so the safe answer is to keep everything
manual.

Most AI consulting answers that with reassurance. This offer answers it with an
architecture: workflows designed so identifying data never enters the model at
all, demonstrated on a live engagement before the client's own data is touched.

That is the thing a 12-year insurance-agency and banking risk background is
actually for, and it is the hardest part of this offer for a generic vendor to
copy.

## What I will not claim

- No ROI number before a measured baseline exists. Hours, manual steps, adoption
  and output quality are measured before and after, or they are not claimed.
- No "AI transformation". One workflow, measurably better, then the next.
- No implied client list. Until there are named references, the proof is the
  shipped products and one anonymised engagement.

## Positioning against the alternatives

| The buyer's real alternative | Why they still lose | The honest counter |
|---|---|---|
| Do it themselves with ChatGPT | The owner's attention is the scarcest asset in a 10-100 person firm. Learning tools comes out of managing, selling and serving. | True for a curious owner with spare evenings. Say so; the buyer respects it. |
| Buy vertical software | Solves the workflow the vendor chose, not the one costing this firm money, and starts a procurement and migration cycle. | Sometimes vertical software is the right answer. Saying that when it is true is what makes the rest credible. |
| Hire an automation freelancer | Automates the process as described rather than as it actually runs, and treats data handling as an afterthought. | This is the closest competitor and the one to beat on process understanding and risk, not on price. |
| A generic AI workshop | Teaches tools, changes no workflow, leaves nothing running on Monday. | The Value Sprint's deliverable is one thing working, not a slide deck. |
