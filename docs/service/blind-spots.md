# Blind-spot map

Written 2026-11-12. The service exists on paper and has not run end to end
with a paying client. This page lists what decides whether it works, what we
actually know, and the cheapest way to find out. No client names, no prices.

Evidence used: the offer, the toolkit, the first engagement (related party, see
[../clients/2026-08-30-michal-engagement-retro.md](../clients/2026-08-30-michal-engagement-retro.md)),
the founder's own trial, and the first arm's-length Discovery call (a retail
business, 2026-11-08, proposal sent 2026-11-11, no answer yet).

## The top 5

1. **What result a client pays for.** The retail client did not ask for "AI
   adoption". She asked for a clear view of the order that is on its way. Her
   main doubt was what we add on top of the business system she already has.
2. **Founder hours per client.** Never measured. The live proposal assumes
   about 15 hours. Pre-sale work (research, deck, six proposal versions) was
   not counted at all.
3. **The value metric and its baseline.** No engagement has one. Without it
   no result can be claimed, and price has nothing to stand on.
4. **Adoption after handover.** Step 6 has never run with anyone.
5. **Which method is the real one.** Three documents describe it (the offer,
   the toolkit, the private sprint guide). Steps 1 to 3 and 7 have no
   template. The [process map](process-map.md) now names one owner per step.

## The full map

tested), **Unknown** (no evidence either way).
