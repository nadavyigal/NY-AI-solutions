# Step 5 — Roadmap

90 days. Walked through live, 45 minutes, not emailed.

## Structure

| Horizon | What | Owner | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1-14 | The Quick Win, running | | Baseline measures re-taken |
| Days 15-45 | The second workflow, plus embedding the first | | Adoption: who uses it unprompted |
| Days 46-90 | The third, plus the review rhythm | | Output quality and preserved human review |

## Required sections

**What we measure.** Restate the step-3 baseline units. Improvement is claimed in
those units or not at all.

**Who owns what.** Every item has a named person on the client side. An item
whose only owner is the consultant does not survive the engagement.

**The review rhythm.** A recurring short review that turns what happened into one
controlled improvement. The first engagement produced this as a standing weekly
review and it is the mechanism that stops an operating system decaying into
unused tools.

**Explicitly out of scope.** The ideas raised and deliberately deferred. This is
the scope-creep control named in EXD-026, and it belongs in writing before
implementation starts, not after.

**What would make us stop.** The conditions under which continuing is the wrong
call. Say them while the relationship is good.
