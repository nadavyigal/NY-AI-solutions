# NY AI Solutions: Service Pitch and Prospect Call Runbook

Use this document to run the first prospect call, decide whether there is a fit,
and move a qualified prospect into an AI Value Sprint. The client-facing service
is simple. Audit Toolkit, Innovation, STORM, and Red Team are internal engines;
they are not four separate products the prospect needs to understand.

## The service in one sentence

**אנחנו לוקחים תהליך עבודה אחד שחוזר על עצמו, בודקים איפה AI יכול לשפר אותו
בלי לאבד שליטה על מידע של לקוחות, ובונים פתרון אחד שעובד בתוך 7–10 ימי עסקים.**

English:

**We take one repeated workflow, determine where AI can improve it without
losing control of client data, and build one working solution in 7–10 business
days.**

## What the client buys

The client does not buy Audit Toolkit, Innovation, STORM, or Red Team.

The client buys the **AI Value Sprint**:

1. A map of current AI subscriptions and actual usage.
2. Three to five real workflows mapped end to end.
3. Opportunities prioritized by value, effort, and risk.
4. One Quick Win built and running.
5. A 90-day plan with measurement defined.

The client-facing sequence is:

```text
Fit call → AI Value Sprint → Implementation and training → Ongoing improvement
```

## How the four internal engines connect

| Engine | Its role | When to run it | What the client sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Audit Toolkit** | The engagement backbone | Always | Intake, workflow map, baseline, audit, roadmap, Quick Win |
| **Innovation / IDEATION** | Opens the solution space and separates symptoms, causes, interventions, and products | Only when the problem or solution space is genuinely uncertain | Several practical alternatives and a justified recommendation |
| **STORM** | Adds perspectives and exposes contradictions or blind spots | When the domain is unfamiliar, strategic, or has multiple stakeholders | Better questions and a more complete analysis; not a branded workshop |
| **Red Team** | Attacks assumptions and failure modes before commitment | Before selecting or implementing a consequential workflow | Risks, safeguards, human controls, and a go/fix/stop decision |

### The operating rule

Audit Toolkit always runs. The other engines are invoked only when the
engagement needs them.

- A simple, well-understood repeated workflow may need no full Innovation run.
- STORM without retrieval produces hypotheses, not facts. High-stakes claims
  must be grounded in current sources.
- Red Team is a gate before commitment, not a performative critique after the
  solution has already been chosen.
- A full eight-step IDEATION run is too large for a normal Value Sprint. Use a
  bounded version unless the client is making a product or strategic investment
  decision rather than improving an operational workflow.

## The three-minute pitch

1. **The situation:** “You probably already have AI licenses, but the important
   work still moves through repeated manual steps.”
2. **The risk:** “For a professional-services firm, value is not enough. We
   also need to know exactly what the model sees, what it never sees, and where
   a person remains responsible.”
3. **The method:** “We observe the workflow, measure the current baseline,
   explore alternatives, stress-test the preferred option, and build one Quick
   Win.”
4. **The deliverable:** “You finish with one workflow running, a prioritized
   opportunity map, and a 90-day plan.”
5. **The first step:** “The fit call should leave us with one named workflow
   worth mapping—or an honest decision that there is no fit right now.”

## The 30–45 minute fit call

### 0–3 minutes: set the contract

Say:

> המטרה שלי היום היא לא למכור לכם מערכת. אני רוצה להבין תהליך אחד שחוזר על
> עצמו, לבדוק אם יש בו הזדמנות אמיתית, ולראות אם יש דרך לעבוד עליו בלי ליצור
> סיכון מידע חדש.

Do not start by sharing the deck. Start with the prospect.

### 3–10 minutes: understand the business

Ask:

1. What does the business sell, and who performs the core work?
2. Which AI subscriptions or automation tools are already paid for?
3. Who uses them, and what do they actually use them for?
4. What has already been tried and abandoned?

### 10–25 minutes: walk one workflow

Ask the prospect to describe the last real occurrence, not the ideal process.

1. What triggers the workflow?
2. Who touches it first?
3. What information enters?
4. Which systems, files, messages, or documents are used?
5. Where does someone copy, retype, wait, chase, check, or correct?
6. Where can an error harm a client or the business?
7. Where is identifying client information present?
8. Who makes the final decision or sends the final output?
9. How often does this happen?
10. What would “better” look like?

Capture four minimum facts:

- Frequency
- Current manual steps or handoffs
- Workflow owner
- Data sensitivity

If these cannot be named, the workflow is not ready for a Sprint proposal.

### 25–32 minutes: reflect the workflow back

Use this format:

> אני שומע שהתהליך מתחיל ב־___, עובר דרך ___ אנשים/מערכות, נתקע בעיקר ב־___,
> ונוגע במידע מסוג ___. השיפור שנראה שווה בדיקה הוא ___, אבל לפני שנציע פתרון
> צריך לוודא ___ ו־___. האם זה מתאר נכון את מה שקורה?

Do not propose a tool yet.

### 32–38 minutes: explain the service with selected slides

Use only the slides needed:

- Slide 2: why existing AI use does not reach workflows.
- Slide 4: the unified method.
- Slide 5: the 7–10 day Sprint.
- Slide 7: privacy architecture, if relevant.
- Slide 9: what the fit call must decide.

Do not automatically present all ten slides.

### 38–45 minutes: make the fit decision

There are three valid outcomes.

#### Qualified for a Sprint

Use when there is:

- A repeated and observable workflow
- A named owner
- Meaningful manual work, delay, inconsistency, or risk
- Access to the people who do the work
- A feasible privacy path to investigate
- A measurable current state

Say:

> יש כאן תהליך ששווה למפות. הצעד הבא שאני מציע הוא AI Value Sprint של 7–10
> ימי עסקים. אני אשלח סיכום של ההיקף, מה נצטרך מכם, ומה יהיה בנוי בסוף.

#### Needs one more piece of evidence

Use when the pain seems real but the workflow, owner, tier, or data path is not
yet clear.

Ask for exactly one missing artifact or one short follow-up with the workflow
owner. Do not turn this into an unpaid audit.

#### No fit now

Use when the issue is not repeated, the prospect wants a general AI workshop,
the workflow cannot be observed, or the main need is a product/IT procurement
project outside the offer.

Say:

> אני לא חושב שספרינט הוא הצעד הנכון כרגע. לפני שבונים משהו, צריך קודם ___;
> אחרת נייצר פרויקט בלי תהליך ברור למדוד.

## How to run the AI Value Sprint

### Step 1: Intake

Use: `docs/toolkit/01-intake.md`

Output:

- Business and workflow boundary
- Existing subscriptions and verified tiers
- People involved
- Candidate workflow pain
- Known data/privacy constraints

### Step 2: Pre-audit

Use: `docs/toolkit/02-pre-audit.md`

Output:

- Current-system inventory
- Public-context research
- Initial hypotheses clearly labeled as hypotheses
- Discovery plan

Run STORM only if an important domain or stakeholder perspective is missing.
Escalate changing or high-stakes claims to grounded research.

### Step 3: Discovery and baseline

Use: `docs/toolkit/03-discovery.md`

Observe the workflow. Record:

- Trigger and finish
- Manual steps
- Handoffs
- Time or volume where measurable
- Rework and errors
- Systems and subscriptions
- Identifying data
- Human-control points

Use bounded Innovation work if the observed problem has multiple plausible root
causes or materially different solution families. Do not run a full IDEATION
process for a straightforward workflow.

### Step 4: Audit and decision

Use: `docs/toolkit/04-audit-report.md`

Produce:

- Findings
- Three to five opportunities
- Value/effort/risk scoring
- A “leave this alone” finding
- Privacy path for each retained opportunity
- Recommended Quick Win

Run Red Team on the recommended Quick Win:

1. Which assumptions are load-bearing?
2. How could this fail operationally?
3. What would make the client feel the solution made work worse?
4. Where could data leak or human review disappear?
5. Verdict: stop, fix then build, or build.

### Step 5: Roadmap

Use: `docs/toolkit/05-roadmap.md`

Output:

- Quick Win scope
- 90-day sequence
- Metrics
- Dependencies
- Risks and safeguards
- Explicit non-goals

### Step 6: Implementation and embedding

Use: `docs/toolkit/06-implementation.md`

Output:

- Quick Win configured or built
- Client validation in the client’s own environment
- Human-review and privacy controls
- Usage instructions
- Re-measurement plan
- Owner and next review date

Book the client configuration session at kickoff. The work is not delivered
until it runs in the client’s account and workflow.

## What success means

Do not claim a return unless the baseline and follow-up use the same boundary.
Measure what fits the workflow:

- Hours or elapsed time
- Manual steps and handoffs
- Rework or error rate
- Output quality
- Employee adoption
- Appropriate human review

One working workflow with credible measurement is a successful Sprint. A long
list of AI ideas is not.
